# Fusion coverage and model conditions

Explicit fusion operations, model roles and compatible evaluation conditions determine inclusion. At this follow-up snapshot, 20 distinct records were added to the corrected 120-record catalogue: 18 method studies, one benchmark and one survey. Following bibliographic curation, the current public corpus contains 139 works, including 12 surveys; CCF applicability is verified for 135 records, including `None` classifications. The focused federated/distributed view contains 52 records, including separately labeled related foundations; this is not a count of 52 deployed federations. Earlier scope exclusions remain in force.

The table follows specific mechanisms rather than federated terminology. FedFomo, FedAMP, FedPAC and FED-PUB construct explicit personalized model/component mixtures; FedDRL's fusion weights, rather than its client scheduling alone, establish inclusion. FedHydra, FedSD2C and FedTAD transfer knowledge from multiple local models, with different released interfaces. MAGDi and AgentMerge consolidate capabilities but do not establish a federated protocol or recursive self-improvement.

## Versions and comparison units

FedHydra uses its final KDD 2025 record. One-Shot Federated Learning is a NeurIPS 2018 companion-workshop paper with a 2019 arXiv upload. GNNMerge and AgentMerge remain workshop records; they do not inherit main-conference ranks. Symmetry-Aware Graph Metanetwork Autoencoders uses PMLR's 2026 publication year while retaining the TAG-DS 2025 event year. Computing and Informatics and TAG-DS were checked against the complete CCF 2026 directory and are not listed.

MergeBench is a full-paper Datasets and Benchmarks contribution: the [official call](https://nips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForDatasetsBenchmarks) states full-paper submission and publication in the NeurIPS proceedings. The catalogue applies the directory's full/regular-paper rule and NeurIPS A rank on that basis. The Practical Guide is an IJCAI Survey Track paper; its CCF classification is shown as `—` because the parent venue alone does not establish the track's applicability.

The [model-condition tables](model-conditions.md) expose seven representative configuration records from five studies. Source, target and auxiliary models are separate. Different student scales, compatible base groups and single-teacher appendix experiments remain separate alternatives, not invented combined experiments. NR records unavailable or unextracted values with a reason; N/A records an inapplicable role. AgentMerge's conflicting appendix parameter label is preserved rather than silently resolved as an author-confirmed correction.

## Search boundary

This pass followed a bounded reference set through official proceedings, author versions and selected method/setup sections; it did not rerun an exhaustive search. GNNMerge's relation to GFFMERGE prompted one direct follow-up, whose separate formal record and mechanism were checked. Exact-title/version searches also resolved the parameter-canonicalization paper. Source locators and access limitations are retained in the master records. AgentMerge method passages were available through the primary-site index while full-PDF access returned 403; section review does not imply complete full-text access. No experiments were reproduced.

The added branches repair gaps in personalized model mixtures, graph-model fusion, generative consolidation and configuration-level comparison. They do not establish literature saturation or justify bringing generic FL methods back into the catalogue.
