# Federated fusion and related foundations

This focused view contains 63 records selected from the master bibliography. Groups distinguish actual federated protocols, decentralized training and related model-reuse foundations; inclusion does not establish privacy, fairness or a real institutional deployment.

See the [federated coverage audit](federated-coverage-audit.md) for the reference-list crosswalk, version reconciliation and disposition of the previous screening queue. [Detailed records](papers.md) retain assumptions, limitations and source locators.
