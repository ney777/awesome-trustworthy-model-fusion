# Model fusion in federated and distributed settings

This focused view contains 52 records with an explicit model-fusion contribution. Each row identifies the models, modules or knowledge being combined and the fusion operation. General federated optimization, client scheduling, gradient defense and protocol security are excluded unless the work directly studies model fusion. Distributed merging foundations are labeled separately; inclusion does not establish privacy, fairness or institutional deployment.

[Detailed records](papers.md) describe assumptions, limitations and source locators. [Model conditions](model-conditions.md) compare selected source, target and auxiliary model configurations. In the CCF column, — means no grade is displayed; None denotes a venue or publication type outside the ranked directory.
