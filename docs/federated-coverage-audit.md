## What caused the gaps

| Observed curation gap | Evidence in this comparison | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete foundation coverage | The baseline lacked FedFisher, FedNova, FedAtt, FedLAW, lo-fi and several train-time composition methods. Recent checkpoint-merging coverage alone did not cover their interfaces. | Add explicit training-time, post-training and distillation foundations; retain related-foundation labels where appropriate. |
| Method aliases and publication changes | FedLAW/FedNova titles omit their familiar method names; FedMerge and Single Global Merging have retitled versions; several arXiv listings now have final AAAI/CVPR/NeurIPS records. | Match titles, aliases, author lists and persistent identifiers; reconcile final metadata without creating duplicate papers. |
| Unseparated federation interfaces | Terminal averaging, progressive block fusion, task-vector weighting, prototype exchange, split gradients and partial-model aggregation expose different information. | Compare the actual combined object, training stage, model compatibility, data access and communication schedule. |
| Reading progress confused with eligibility | Buffalo has a publisher abstract; FairFHTL has verified identity plus an author synopsis. Neither warrants invented method details. | Keep relevant records visible while recording the exact reading depth and unresolved article-track applicability. |

These are demonstrated coverage and curation weaknesses, not proof that every missing title was absent from every earlier search. The comparison does not estimate overall recall. Shared authors, healthcare applications and the word “fusion” are not sufficient inclusion criteria; each added work has an identified model, representation, update, composition or governance object.
