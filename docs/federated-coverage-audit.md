## Correction and version boundaries

The earlier search repaired real omissions in matching, distillation and merging, but expanded from those mechanisms into general federated optimization, gradient combination and protocol security. The correction separates these two outcomes: source and version reconciliation remains useful, while inclusion is reconsidered against the actual research contribution. Neither the phrase “federated learning” nor a source list's category heading is sufficient.

| Issue | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| General FL optimization or scheduling | FedAvg, FedNova, HeteroFL and FedRolex are excluded from this crosswalk's included subset. Their role as useful background elsewhere does not make them fusion records here. |
| Generic gradient or protocol analysis | FedBone and Breaking Privacy in Model-Heterogeneous FL are excluded; task-gradient combination and secure-aggregation leakage are insufficient on their own. |
| Insufficient direct-fusion evidence | Buffalo and FairFHTL are excluded from the counted library while their evidence and screening decisions remain available. |
| Explicit fusion-weight methods | FedLAW, FedAWA, FedAtt and LIVAR remain for their inspected model/layer combination operators, with the rationale made explicit in `fusion_relevance`. |
| Model fusion with further training | DapperFL, FuseFL and LSS remain because blending, block composition or model-soup preparation is central. Training-free operation is not required. |
| Publication aliases | FedMerge and Single Global Merging each have one canonical identity; their older titles stay as source aliases. |
| Formal versions | FedMerge, FedBip and FedEM use AAAI 2026; FedAWA uses CVPR 2025; FuseFL and LSS use NeurIPS 2024; task-arithmetic FL uses TMLR 2025. |
| Incomplete proceedings or distinct tracks | Potara is NeurIPS 2026 accepted with proceedings pending. MaTU is a companion workshop and does not inherit IJCAI A. |
| Preprint status | Wasserstein-barycenter fusion, FedSEAM and IsoLoCo remain preprints; no final venue is inferred. |
| Existing named seeds | Model Trip remains final-abstract-reviewed; PrivFusion's mechanism account remains tied to its identified author version. Their fusion relevance is direct despite these reading limits. |

The separate scope report also explains the ten older exclusions and the removal of three comparison surveys plus MoFedNet from the focused method view. The surveys remain in Related Surveys; MoFedNet remains in model collaboration/evolution. They are not promoted to fusion methods to preserve a larger table count.

The crosswalk does not estimate recall or establish coverage of all federated model-fusion literature. Technical summaries retain data access, communication, training and compatibility assumptions. Repeated gossip is not one total communication event, local data retention is not a privacy guarantee, and task/client fairness is not demographic fairness. Source-section review is not experimental reproduction or a full proof audit.

The previous AFL-CS author-page lead remains uncounted. Its asynchronous-training/aggregation title does not by itself justify a model-fusion record. Private manuscripts, downloaded full texts and source excerpts remain outside this public repository.
