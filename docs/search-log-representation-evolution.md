# Representation and evolution discovery log

The useful distinction in this corpus is between internal alignment, external capability organization, input-dependent composition, and actual changes across successive model arrivals or user interactions. Transferability scores and task embeddings supply selection infrastructure; they do not establish that two models can be fused safely. Routing and dynamic merging adapt decisions to an input, whereas continual merging and evolving learnware specifications explicitly update an accumulated state.

This is a targeted, source-verified discovery set for Phase 1, not an exhaustive or systematic review. Search date: **2026-11-07**. Working scope: 2018–2026 plus necessary foundations. This discovery batch contains 41 distinct records: 26 primarily representation and 15 primarily evolution; 5 are related foundations and 4 are benchmark/tool resources. Of these, 30 are dated 2024–2026. All 41 records have selected primary-text sections inspected; this label does not mean every proof, appendix, or experiment was fully reviewed or reproduced.

## Sources and retrieval

Discovery used web searches restricted where possible to PMLR, NeurIPS proceedings, ICLR proceedings/OpenReview, CVF, ACL Anthology, IJCAI, AAAI, Nature, DBLP, arXiv, and author/institutional pages. Publisher or official conference metadata controls the final title, author list, venue, year, and publication type. DBLP and author publication pages supplied secondary bibliographic cross-checks when OpenReview downloads were blocked. Technical claims were taken from primary PDFs, not DBLP or third-party summaries.

Public PDFs were downloaded and text-extracted with `pypdf`. Downloaded source snapshots remain private; each PDF extraction includes explicit `[PDF page N]` markers. Metadata snapshots retain the requested URL, discovered PDF URL, citation meta tags, retrieval status, and relevant links. Publisher page upload dates are distinct from conference years. Public code links were accepted only when their provenance was a primary paper/page or the authors’ repository; a separate HTTP reachability check records availability without claiming code execution or reproducibility.

Direct OpenReview PDF requests for Git Re-Basin, Task Arithmetic, RegMean, RouteLLM, and RegMean++ were blocked. Their author arXiv versions were retrieved, and the record states that version distinction. AAAI’s direct endpoint returned an access challenge to the local downloader; the final ELSI paper was read through the web PDF reader and a nine-page author-hosted copy. MergeKit and Kinship HTML snapshots lacked usable citation metadata, so official ACL web retrieval supplied metadata and direct official PDF links supplied method evidence. Beimingwu’s first author link returned 404; a second author-hosted final ten-page KDD PDF succeeded. These access failures are not evidence that a paper or venue is absent.

## Citation expansion actually performed

Backward inspection used the related-work and reference sections of LEEP, PLeaS, DOP, NUFILT, ProbSurgery, and the learnware papers. These connect LEEP to task embeddings, PLeaS to permutation alignment, DOP/NUFILT to OPCM and earlier continual merging, ProbSurgery to Surgery, and Beimingwu/label-space assembly to ELSI. Forward follow-up was performed with named-method searches and the official later papers: RegMean → RegMean++; Surgery → ProbSurgery; OPCM/DOP → NUFILT and ODE-M; task/model specifications → ELSI and label-space assembly. This was **targeted forward citation follow-up, not a complete cited-by graph export**; citation-graph saturation is not claimed.

Deduplication used canonical title, final official URL/DOI, author sequence, and version identity. A preprint and its final publication count once. Final records retain separate preprint/version notes when they materially differ. Each paper has exactly one primary category; benchmark/tool and foundation status are separate scope labels and should not be mistaken for additional papers.

## Corrections and evidence boundaries

- **Task Arithmetic:** final ICLR/DBLP metadata has six authors; arXiv v3 has seven, adding Suchin Gururangan. The final six-author record is used, and the discrepancy is preserved.
- **RouteLLM:** the final ICLR/DBLP title uses “from Preference Data”; the inspected arXiv version uses “with Preference Data.” The formal title is retained.
- **OPCM:** the final NeurIPS2025 title is *Merging on the Fly Without Retraining: A Sequential Approach to Scalable Continual Model Merging*. Its final eight-author list includes Lefei Zhang. The publisher upload date is in 2026; that does not change the conference year.
- **FusionBench:** final JMLR 26(307):1–38, 2025 has eight authors. The earlier arXiv title/author list is not substituted for it.
- **RegMean++:** the inspected revised author PDF states TMLR publication in April 2026; the record is not left as an unqualified 2025 preprint.
- **Publication tracks:** MergeKit is EMNLP Industry, Kinship is Findings EMNLP, Beimingwu is KDD Applied Data Science. These do not silently inherit a main/research-track CCF label. Paper-level CCF classifications are stored in the master database; unknown track applicability is displayed as `—`.
- **Calibration is not optional metadata:** AdaMerging and Surgery use unlabeled test examples; ProbSurgery uses unlabeled test/validation examples; PLeaS-free uses public calibration data; RegMean-family methods require data-derived statistics; ODE-M requires calibration loss gradients. None should be casually called “data-free” on the strength of not pooling raw training data.
- **Evolution claims differ:** RouteLLM, Twin-Merging and REMEDY perform input-dependent selection/composition. Evolutionary recipe optimization is offline search. OPCM/DOP/NUFILT maintain a merged state over model arrivals. ODE-M additionally uses calibration feedback. ELSI refines model specifications and indexes during market growth and user interactions.
- **Model Kinship has an explicit negative boundary:** its final Limitations section, PDF p.9, says the current approach does not support sustained evolution; environmental feedback and reward mechanisms are future work.
- **ELSI’s model-combination boundary:** §3 studies single-model identification and leaves multi-model details for future work. The later IJCAI2025 label-space paper actually studies combination/assembly and requires local labeled data in its reuse phase.
- **Trust claims:** raw-data non-sharing, capability similarity, and accuracy/cost improvements do not by themselves prove differential privacy, fairness, calibration, adversarial robustness, or safe deployment. Such guarantees were not invented for this corpus.

## Remaining review limits

All reported method/limitation notes are selected-section assessments. They do not certify implementation correctness, repeat experiments, validate claimed effect sizes, or exhaustively assess proofs. Code reachability and paper provenance do not establish that the software works. The integrated repository separately records CCF checks, duplicate checks, and the governance extension. Agent collaboration, rollback, model retirement, and composable trust constraints remain sparse here; no generic agent paper was inserted solely to fill those labels. Citation-key/BibTeX generation must preserve the formal author lists and version notes above.
